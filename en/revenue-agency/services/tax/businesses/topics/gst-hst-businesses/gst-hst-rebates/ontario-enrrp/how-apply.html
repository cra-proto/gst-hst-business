Ontario enhanced new residential rental property (ENRRP) rebate

How to apply

How you apply for the Ontario ENRRP rebate depends on whether you purchased a rental property (purchaser/landlord) or constructed a rental property (builder/landlord), the type of housing, and the arrangement under which the property is rented.

On this page

  • Determine your application type and filing deadline
  • Fill out and submit your forms

Determine your application type and filing deadline

There are 5 application types to choose from. Each application type has its own filing deadline.

The filing deadline generally depends on when tax first became payable on the purchase of the property or when tax was deemed to have been paid on a self-supply.

Application type 6: Lease of building and land

It depends

If you are a purchaser/landlord

You must submit the application

A purchaser/landlord must submit the application for the Ontario ENRRP rebate directly to the CRA. A builder can not pay or credit the rebate to the purchaser/landlord.

When to submit

You must file your application within two years after the end of the month in which tax first became payable on your purchase

When tax becomes payable

If you purchased a residential condominium unit and possession was transferred before the condominium complex was registered as a condominium, tax becomes payable on the earlier of:

  • The day ownership of the unit is transferred to you
  • The day that is 60 days after the condominium complex is registered

In any other case, tax becomes payable on the earlier of:

  • The day ownership of the property is transferred to you
  • The day possession of the property is transferred to you under the agreement of purchase and sale
If you are a builder/landlord

When to submit

You must file your application within two years after the end of the month in which the self-supply occurred.

When the self-supply occurs

If you are a builder who constructed or substantially renovated a residential complex, made an addition to a multiple unit residential complex, or converted non-residential real property into a residential complex, the self-supply occurs on the later of:

  • The day construction or substantial renovation is substantially completed
  • The day possession or use of the residential complex, or a unit in the complex or addition, is first given under a lease, licence or similar arrangement for residential occupancy
Application type 7: Sale of building and lease of land

When to submit

You must file your application within two years after the end of the month in which the self-supply occurred.

When the self-supply occurs

The self-supply occurs on the later of:

  • The day the construction or substantial renovation of the residential complex is substantially completed
  • The day you give possession or use of the housing under an agreement for the exempt sale of the housing and exempt lease of the related land
Application type 8: Co-operative housing corporation (co-op)

When to submit

Whether you are a co-op purchaser/landlord or a co-op builder/landlord, you must file your application within two years after the end of the month in which you lease the residential unit as a place of residence for individuals.

Application type 9A or 9B: Lease of land

When to submit

You must file your application within two years after the end of the month in which the self-supply occurred.

When the self-supply occurs

Generally, if you made an exempt lease of land for residential use, the self-supply occurs:

  • On the day the first lease begins
  • On the day a change in use occurs that results in a deemed self-supply

For more information on the self-supply rules that apply to land, refer to:

  • What is self-supply?
  • GST/HST Memorandum 19-2-3, Residential Real Property – Deemed Supplies

Fill out and submit your forms

Intro

Online

Electronic filing is not currently available for this rebate. Updates to allow electronic filing are expected in fall 2026. Until then, applications must be submitted by mail.

By mail

  1. Fill out your forms, including the consent sections for the ONHAP to allow the CRA to share information with the Province of Ontario.
    Depending on your application type, complete:
    • Form GST524, GST/HST New Residential Rental Property Rebate Application
    • Form RC7524-ON, Ontario Rebate Schedule
    • Form GST525, Supplement to the New Residential Rental Property Rebate Application – Co-op and Multiple Units
  2. Send the completed forms and all required supporting documents to the address indicated on the application form.

For detailed instructions, refer to: Guide RC4231, GST/HST New Residential Rental Property Rebate
