Ontario enhanced new residential rental property (ENRRP) rebate

Who can apply

Intro

On this page

  • Eligibility criteria

Eligibility criteria

Intro

You lease both the building and land

You may be eligible if all the following apply:

  • You are eligible for the Ontario NRRP rebate
  • Construction or substantial renovation is substantially completed on or before December 31, 2029
  • Tax becomes payable on or before December 31, 2032
  • The fair market value of the housing is less than $1.85 million

You must also meet the following criteria depending on whether you are a purchaser or a builder:

If you are a purchaser/landlord

You are a purchaser/landlord if you paid the GST/HST on the purchase of newly constructed or substantially renovated housing, or on the purchase of an interest in the housing, and leased the housing or residential units in the housing to another person for long-term residential use by an individual.

You maybe be eligible if one of the following applies:

  • Construction or substantial renovation began on or after April 1, 2026, and on or before March 31, 2027

  • Construction or substantial renovation began on or before March 31, 2026, and you entered into an agreement of purchase and sale for a single unit residential complex or residential condominium unit on or after April 1, 2026, and on or before March 31, 2027

If you are a builder/landlord

You are a builder/landlord if reported and remitted the GST/HST on a self-supply of newly constructed or substantially renovated housing or an addition to a multiple unit residential complex and subsequently leased the housing or units for long-term residential use by an individual.

You may be eligible if:

  • Construction or substantial renovation began on or after April 1, 2026, and on or before March 31, 2027
You are a co-operative housing corporation

You may be eligible if all the following apply:

  • You are eligible for the Ontario NRRP rebate
  • Construction or substantial renovation is substantially completed on or before December 31, 2029
  • Tax becomes payable on or before December 31, 2032
  • The fair market value of the housing is less than $1.85 million

You must also meet the following criteria depending on whether you are a purchaser or a builder:

If you are a co-op purchaser/landlord

You are a co-op purchaser/landlord if you are a cooperative housing corporation that paid GST/HST on the purchase of newly constructed or substantially renovated housing, or on the purchase of an interest in the housing from a builder and leased a residential unit for long-term residential use by an individual.

You maybe be eligible if one of the following applies:

  • Construction or substantial renovation began on or after April 1, 2026, and on or before March 31, 2027

  • Construction or substantial renovation began on or before March 31, 2026, and you entered into an agreement of purchase and sale for a single unit residential complex on or after April 1, 2026, and on or before March 31, 2027

If you are a co-op builder/landlord

You are a co-op builder/landlord if you are a cooperative housing corporation that reported and remitted the GST/HST on a self-supply of housing that is a residential complex or an addition to a multiple unit residential complex and leased residential units in the housing for long-term residential use.

You may be eligible if:

  • Construction or substantial renovation began on or after April 1, 2026, and on or before March 31, 2027
You sell the building and lease the land

You are a builder who reported and remitted the GST/HST on a self-supply of housing or an addition to a multiple unit residential complex and made an exempt sale of the building together with an exempt long-term lease of the land under a single written agreement.

You may be eligible if all the following apply:

  • You are eligible for the Ontario NRRP rebate
  • The purchaser of the building is eligible for the GST/HST new housing rebate
  • Construction or substantial renovation began on or after April 1, 2026, and on or before March 31, 2027
  • Construction or substantial renovation is substantially completed on or before December 31, 2029
  • Tax becomes payable on or before December 31, 2032
  • The fair market value of the housing is less than $1.85 million
You sell the building and lease the land

You reported and remitted the GST/HST on a self-supply at the time of the first lease or the time of a change in use of the land and the supply is one of the following:

  • A lease of land, other than a site in a residential trailer park, which provides continuous possession for a person in occupation or possession of a residential unit affixed to that land and used as a place of residence for individuals
  • A lease of a site in a residential trailer park for a person in occupation or possession of a mobile home, travel trailer, motor home or similar vehicle or trailer

You may be eligible if all the following apply:

  • You are eligible for the Ontario NRRP rebate
  • Tax is deemed to have been paid on or after April 1, 2026, and on or before March 31, 2027
  • The fair market value or basic tax content of the land is less than $610,500

Restrictions

The following restrictions apply where eligibility for the Ontario ENRRP rebate is based, in part, on an agreement of purchase and sale entered into between April 1, 2026, and March 31, 2027.

Assignment sales
For assignment sales, to be eligible for the Ontario ENRRP rebate, both the initial agreement of purchase and sale with the builder and the assignment agreement must be entered into between April 1, 2026, and March 31, 2027.
Change in agreement
If you entered into an agreement with a builder before April 1, 2026, and you make changes to the agreement such that it is considered to be entered into on or after April 1, 2026, for the purposes of eligibility for the Ontario ENRRP rebate, the agreement is deemed to have been entered into before April 1, 2026, and you are not entitled to the rebate.
New agreement
If you entered into an agreement with a builder before April 1, 2026, the agreement is terminated and another agreement is entered into on or after April 1, 2026, to be eligible for the Ontario ENRRP rebate, the new agreement must have been entered into for bona fide purposes other than to obtain the Ontario ENRRP rebate.
