Ontario enhanced new residential rental property (ENRRP) rebate

The Ontario enhanced new residential rental property (ENRRP) rebate is temporarily available to eligible landlords in addition to the Ontario new residential rental property (NRRP) rebate.

The Ontario ENRRP is available between April 1, 2026, and March 31, 2027.

Landlords who are eligible for the Ontario ENRRP rebate may also be eligible for the Ontario new home affordability payment (ONHAP), administered by the Province of Ontario.
